When do the paid plans open?

No date yet. When they do, how billing, renewal and cancellation work is already written down in the terms of service, so none of it will be new on the day.

How will I be billed?

Through Google Play or the App Store, not by us. The store holds your payment details and issues the receipt, and you cancel there too. We never see your card.

What happens if I cancel?

The paid features run to the end of the period you have already paid for, then stop. Cancelling never cuts a period short.

Does the free tier expire?

No. It is not a trial. Logging, streaks, charts and the catalogue are free permanently, and adding paid tiers has never removed anything from it.

Why is Valhalla so much more?

Because a person does the work. Form check and nutrition coaching are hours of someone’s time rather than server capacity, which is also why places are limited.
