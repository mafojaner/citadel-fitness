Every release that reaches a device gets an entry here, including the ones only testers can install. If a bug you reported is fixed, this is where it will say so.

1.0.0 3 September 2026 · build 3 Internal testing

The first build to leave a laptop. Android only, on the internal testing track, which means it is installable by invited testers and is not on the public store.

What is in it

  • Workout logging against a catalogue of 273 exercises, with sets, reps, weight, distance, duration and effort.
  • Streaks, weekly volume and activity charts, with backdated sessions marked as backdated.
  • Weights and distances in kg or lb and km or mi, converted rather than added together.
  • Activity leaderboard, ranked by days trained in the last seven, same-day logs only.
  • The newsletter, with per-category notifications.
  • Data export as CSV, and account deletion that runs immediately from inside the app.

Known limits

  • No paid plans. Nothing can be bought and no payment details are asked for anywhere.
  • Crash reports from this build arrive without source maps, so they are harder to read than they will be. It does not affect the app itself.
  • iOS is not built yet.
Before 1.0.0 August 2026 Not released

Everything before build 3 ran on a development server rather than on anyone's phone, so there is nothing to list: no version of Citadel Fitness was installable before September.

That is deliberately said rather than dressed up with a list of pre-release milestones. A changelog that includes work nobody could use is a progress report wearing a changelog's clothes.
