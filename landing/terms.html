Your content

Your training data is yours. Every workout, set, goal, note, photo and video you put into the app belongs to you, and nothing here changes that.

To run the service we need permission to store and process it: to show your history back to you, to calculate your streaks and charts, to show a coach the video you submitted for review, and to back it up. That permission exists only for operating the app, ends when you delete the content or your account, and does not extend to anything else. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to train anything.

Some of what you enter is deliberately visible to others. Your display name, photo and days trained appear on the activity leaderboard to every signed-in user, and the same within any group you join. What you lifted is never shown. If you would rather not appear, do not set a display name or photo.

You can take a copy of your data out of the app, and you can delete it. Both are described in the privacy policy, and deletion has its own page.

06

Not medical advice

Read this one properly

Citadel Fitness is a training log and a set of suggestions built from it. It is not medical advice, and nobody involved in it is your doctor. Exercise carries risk of injury, and lifting the number an app suggests is your decision and your responsibility.

Speak to a doctor before starting or changing a training programme, particularly if you have an injury, a heart condition, are pregnant, or are recovering from illness. Stop if something hurts.

Where the app suggests a load, that suggestion is a readable heuristic and nothing more: double progression, with a brake when your reported effort is high and a deload when you stall. It reads what you logged. It does not know how you slept, what else you did today, or what your body is doing. Every suggestion is one you should be willing to argue with.

Coaching features

Some paid features involve a person reading what you sent and replying: form check, where someone watches a video of a lift and comments on it, and nutrition coaching, where someone reads what you have described and writes back.

These are opinions offered in good faith by someone experienced in training. They are not a diagnosis, a prescription, a meal plan issued by a dietitian, or physiotherapy. Section 06 applies to them in full.

Both have limits that exist because a person does the work, and those limits are enforced by the app rather than being a target: form check is capped monthly, and nutrition coaching allows one open intake at a time. We aim to reply within a reasonable period and do not guarantee a turnaround time.

Paid plans

Nothing is on sale yet. The app is free to use today, and every paid feature is either unavailable or clearly marked. This section describes how paid plans will work when they open, so it is written down before anyone is charged rather than afterwards.

How you will be billed

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store, not by us directly. The store takes the payment, holds your payment details, and issues the receipt. We never see your card.

A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period, at the price shown when you bought it, until you cancel. That is how the stores work and it will be stated on the purchase screen too.

Cancelling

Cancel through the store, not through us. Google Play and the App Store each own their subscription settings, and we cannot cancel on your behalf. The app links you to the right place.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not end the period you have already paid for: your paid features stay until that period runs out, and then stop. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel first, or you may keep being charged for an account that no longer exists.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by whichever store took the payment, under that store's policy. We cannot issue one for a purchase we did not process. If a store refunds you, the paid features end.

Prices and what you get

Prices are shown in the app in your own currency before you buy. We may change prices for future periods, and if we do, the change applies from your next renewal and never retroactively.

Paid features may be added, changed or withdrawn. If we withdraw something substantial that you are paying for, we will tell you and you can cancel.
